Options compared Simulation

The four answers to the program decision, priced from one set of your figures. The days behind every amount are under "Effort in days" further down.

How firm is the answer?

Your figures are estimates. This shows which of them could change the order of the options if they were wrong, and how wrong they would have to be.

Exact distances, as computed

    Each distance is solved from your figures, not sampled at a chosen spread. It says where the order flips, not how likely that is.

    Effort in days

    Proposed from the 552 lines with fixed factors per 1,000 lines that nothing measured. A proposal counts only once you take it over. Change any figure here and it becomes yours; every amount above moves with it.

    How it is calculatedFactors, formula, what is not priced

    The cost of an option = one-off effort (low and high) + effort per release × releases in the period + the yearly upkeep for Keep × years — every developer day at your developer day rate, every test and key-user day at your key-user day rate. Rounded only where it is shown.

    Ranges stay ranges. An option is the lowest only when its upper bound is below every other option's lower bound. In the header, amounts are rounded outward to two significant figures (the lower bound down, the upper bound up), so a rounded range is never narrower than the computed one; the table shows whole units.
