  • The walk-through as an invitation, never a lock; the soft gate is one calm note (ADR-118).
  • The score is a grade, not a compliance percentage; nothing from a model is in it; nothing is green (ADR-093).
  • Model summary and technical detail stay folded and marked (owner decision 02.10.2026).
  • The two charts, not three: "The rules it applies" waits for N6 (AB-8, later).
