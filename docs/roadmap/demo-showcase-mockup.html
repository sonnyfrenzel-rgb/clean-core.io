    All together (default) next to a lens

    The owner's summary: one story for all three readers, like one project with three views. "All together" is the default; a lens only shifts the emphasis and the closing call, it never hides the other two lines. Two chapters, four ways.

    The start: lens first, or all together?

    Rejected · lens as a first choice

    "I'm from the business side / from IT / from management"

    A question before any value. A reader who is two of the three, or forwards it, has to choose wrongly. It also implies the other views are hidden, which the product does not do.

    Recommended · all together, lens optional

    The story starts at once; "Read it as" sits quietly in the progress bar

    Same as the product's view switch: Business · IT · Management, in that order (ADR-044). A shared link can carry a lens (/demo#lens=it), so "send this to your IT colleague" opens with IT emphasised.

    Conversion: from a click to a real user

    Owner, 13.11.2026: "look closely at how we can get even better from clicks to real users." Within the binding rules: sign-up and account unchanged, no third-party tracking, a signed-out visit contacts only its own origin.

    Hypotheses, and what each would move

    CTA wording: where a real choice exists

    What to measure, without a third party · owner and lawyer decision

    Nothing below is presumed or built. Today the page counts nothing. Any count needs the owner's go and the lawyer's view, and the privacy notice would need a line before the first count is taken.

    What it means legally (for the conversation with the lawyer, not a legal assessment)

    • No cookie and nothing written to the visitor's device: the counts are increments on the server when the page asks its own origin for a chapter. A design without device access avoids the consent question for the device; whether a server-side count of a request is processing of personal data depends on whether IP or user agent is kept, and the proposal keeps neither.
    • The request itself still carries the IP to Cloud Run as every page request does; the count must not log it beyond what the platform already logs.
    • The privacy notice needs one sentence: which counts, that they are aggregated per day, no cookie, no profile, retention.
    • Product-side counts (sign-up, first project, first analysis) already exist as data; reading them as daily totals adds no new processing, but linking a sign-up to a showcase visit would. The proposal does not link them.
