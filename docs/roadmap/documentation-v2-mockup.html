What it already gets right — kept here unchanged

  • No inputs, the RACI read-only, sign-off left to the company (ADR-107 (6), (5)).
  • Answer first: the process in two sentences with start and end (ADR-107 (1)).
  • The step page: one sentence, then Decides · Stops when · Reads · Changes · Who, code on a click (ADR-107 (3), (4)). It is the best block of that mock-up and stays as it is.
  • Evidence is quiet, "Not determined" is first class; Export is the one primary action with What goes in and Left to your own standard.
  • Controls in the code (now beside the rules they repeat, chapter 2); revisions and the Level D register folded into the appendix; no engine change, no stored field, no rules change.

For the owner

In one sentence

Documentation becomes the document itself, one page for every reader, in the order of the Word file: an answer a manager can stop at (what it does, where it starts and ends, what needs a decision, four numbers, ready to hand over) with the export beside it; then Analyze's map as Figure 1 with Analyze's four phases and step names, the chapters and the appendix. What a developer needs — technical names, the code, every table and call, IT's own questions — is there on a click. Nothing to fill in.

Decisions

  1. D1 One page in the export's order. The five tabs, "You are here" and the depth meter go; the one "On this page" bar comes back, built exactly to the shared spec of 15.11.2026 (the same bar on every tool, PageAnchorBar): six items in page order — How it works, Rules, When it stops, What it changes, Who does what, Open questions 6 — and Export as its one right-end action. The chapter numbers in the headings show where the reader is; the "You are reading …" label of v2 is gone. Recommended: yes. Alternative: keep ADR-107's tabs and only apply D2–D7.
  2. D2 Analyze's steps, names and phases. 13 steps of the map grouped in Analyze's four phases, numbered once; ADR-107's 11 tiles go. The step page of ADR-107 stays, opened in place. Recommended: yes.
  3. D3 Figure 1 is Analyze's map, read-only — on the page and in Word/Confluence/PDF; processOverviewSvg retires (Markdown keeps a text outline). No editing here: the map editor stays in Analyze. Recommended: yes. Alternative: keep the one-column overview in the files and show the map only on screen (cheaper, but the reader gets a different picture).
  4. D4 Export beside the answer, the formats visible without opening anything, Word first; sticky in the bar. Recommended: yes.
  5. D5 Four numbers in the answer: steps, ways a case ends, business rules, open questions — the counts Analyze shows, each with its source. Recommended: yes.
  6. D6 RACI stays read-only (ADR-107 (6) over the 10.10 rule "editable by the owner"; the later decision holds). New: the rows are the four roles read from the code, the columns the five parts of the process; only the letters are the model's proposal, and A is left to the company's standard. An edit already on record is still shown. Recommended: yes.
  7. D7 One list of questions: Analyze's six business questions, same wording and numbers; IT's four in the IT view; answered in the project's one list. Recommended: yes.
  8. D8 One view for every reader — decided by the owner, 15.11.2026 (“nur eine Sicht für alle — auch bei Documentation”). No IT or Management variant, and no view switch on this tool (owner, same day: “wenn es der gleiche view ist müssen wir den umschalter auch nicht anzeigen, das verwirrt nur”): where the other tools show Business · IT · Management, Documentation shows only the quiet note “One page for every reader”, on desktop and phone. The view chosen elsewhere is kept and applies again on the next tool. Management is served by the first screen (answer, “Needs a decision”, four numbers, “Ready to hand over”); IT by technical names on hover and in the step page, the code on a click, chapter 5’s full table and IT’s four questions as folds. Decided.
  9. D9 One step count everywhere. The workspace tile mock-up says "94 steps" for this program, Analyze v4 has 29 elements and 13 tasks on the top level. One function, used by the tile, Analyze and Documentation, must decide before the build. Recommended: tasks on the map's top level, sub-process steps counted in the appendix.
  10. D10 Delete components/DocumentSection.tsx (no importer) with DOC-N6. Recommended: yes.
