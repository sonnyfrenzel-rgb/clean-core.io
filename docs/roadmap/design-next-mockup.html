What Design reads from Economics and Management

  • The decision record (project.decision, lib/project-decision.ts): option, status (draft · confirmed · withdrawn · outdated with reasons), who confirmed it and when.
  • Its cost basis (decision.costBasis, ADR-092): the Economics revision it was priced on and which option was cheapest there. Design prints the sentence, never an amount (ADR-022), and links to Economics.
  • The guard rail: the decision binds the architecture contract's fingerprint (ADR-085). When the design's route departs from the bound contract (a declared deviation), or the decision is outdated, Design says "This design lies outside what Economics priced — re-price" with the link. Design computes no amount.
  • Nothing is written back and no field is added.
