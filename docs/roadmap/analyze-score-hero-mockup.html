Analyze: the Clean Core Score as the hero, and declutter wave 2

What you are asked to approve. Three things, each on its own:

  1. The hero (pins H1–H6): the score becomes the first block of Analyze, full width, under the stage header — the number with its band on the one scale, what moved it as a table of deductions in plain words with line links, the edition rule in one line, and where the figure comes from (signed run, engine, rules). It replaces the answer header's three facets, the status line and the separate score section.
  2. Wave 2 as listed beside each frame — every block Analyze shows today, marked kept, folded into … or removed, each with its reason. Nothing signed changes; nothing leaves the product that is not said once elsewhere.
  3. Answers to the five open questions at the end of the list (other edition's figure, the largest-deduction line, complexity/criticality, the inventory tables, one "Add evidence" menu).

Honesty rules kept on every frame: the score is "a grade, not a compliance percentage — and not an SAP measure"; band names and ranges are the ones of lib/clean-core-score.ts; the 20 points for what the engine could not assess stay visible as Not determined, never folded away; the model's text keeps Model proposal; no green on the score (green means proven).

Case: Z_MM_PO_APPROVAL (starter example, 668 lines) as an own project with target Private Edition · engine v3.0.9 · rules levels 3c38976c5841 · figures measured 11.10.2026 with scoreBreakdown() under both rules: Private 37, Public 23 · 31 places / 25 findings · Critical 2 · High 3 · Medium 21 · Low 4 · Info 1 · levels A 0 · B 2 · C 19 · D 2 · Unknown 4 (27 of 31 places) · route Side-by-Side on SAP BTP · 8 places in 4 kinds not assessed · run id and date are illustrative.

Wave 2 — what happens to each block Analyze shows today

Measured against the live page (3.0.9) and analyze/page.tsx. Pins refer to the frames.

Kept

  • Stage header, tools bar, "Back to project workspace"ADR-050/060/063 — the same frame on every tool.
  • "On this page" bar K1ADR-091; it now starts under the hero, so its first anchor is "Look here first", and "Clean Core Score 62" leaves it — the score is the page's top.
  • Look here first K2The three findings to read first, with code — the step from score to code.
  • Where in the programThe only picture of where in 668 lines the findings sit.
  • Findings by kind (Kind · Severity · Line) K3The complete list; gains a Points column so it adds up to the hero.
  • Route card with "Why this route" and "Decide in Management"Wave 1's one route card; the decision stays in Management (ADR-083 a).
  • Model summary (folded, Model proposal) and Technical detail (folded) K5Outside the signature by design; one action deeper.
  • Stale strip and the "run made with a different engine" noticeMove into the hero: when the deductions do not add up to the signed score, the table gives way to that one sentence and "Run the analysis again".
  • "Understanding Clean Core" dialogOpened by the hero's "?" — the one place that explains all four bands.

Folded into the hero

  • Score facet in the answer header → H1The score stood three times (facet, section, anchor chip). Now once.
  • Score section with four band cards → scale + band names (H1), full sentences behind "?"Four cards of 6–8 lines each took a screen to say what one labelled scale says; nothing lost, the dialog has every sentence.
  • "What moves it" paragraph → deduction table H5Today it is prose and, on a mismatch, says nothing. The table names each kind in plain words, its lines and its points.
  • Answer sentence ("3 findings to review — 1 high … the engine read all N lines without a model")→ the lead under the stage title and H4 "no model involved"; the counts go to F1.
  • Status line Evidence · Run · Target · Successors → H3/H4, F3Run, engine and target are the score's provenance; "Successors n of m" goes to the table's column, where it is per kind.
  • "Details" button (file, lines, catalog, engine) → H4Said once, beside the figure it describes.
  • Findings and Level distribution facets → F1, F2Kept as figures, one row under the hero, with severity words and every level letter.
  • "Not determined" side card → F4 + one side card K4The 20 points stand in the hero as Not determined; the card keeps one line and the link to ADR-081's one list.
  • WhyScorePanel (Technical detail)Its route rationale is the route card's, its score is the hero; nothing of its own is left.
  • "Add usage data" + "Add ATC results" → one "Add evidence ▾" W8Two ghost buttons for one kind of act; ?add=usage|atc keeps opening the dialog directly (ADR-081).
  • "Export Confluence" → one "Export ▾" W9ADR-078/082's Export menu, as on Documentation and Design.

Removed

  • Complexity and criticality meters (Technical detail)Two more 1–10 scores beside the one score; "criticality — business impact … mission-critical" is a heuristic the code cannot prove. Needs your go (Q3).
  • Code inventory table, module heatmap, data coupling tableSaid once in the IT view's "Objects & dependencies" (ADR-086); a link stays in Technical detail. Needs your go (Q4).
  • CoverageVerdict explanation texts ("statically verified … map 1:1 to released standard models", "kernel calls … must be decommissioned")Claims no rule of the engine makes; the construct list stays, its three labels get the words of the support matrix.

  • Q4 Inventory and data coupling only in IT, with a link from Technical detail?
  • Q5 One "Add evidence ▾" menu instead of two buttons?
