Every decision that `DESIGN.md` sets, with date, occasion and reasoning. `DESIGN.md` says what applies; this log
says why. A new colour, a new radius, a new pattern or a deviation from one gets an entry here
before it is in `DESIGN.md`. Entries are not deleted; a decision that has been overtaken is marked as *superseded by*.
